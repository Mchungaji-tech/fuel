# Sarura Fuel Logistics

## Executive Summary

Sarura Fuel Logistics is a business management platform for a Kenyan fuel transport company. It helps the business manage drivers, trucks, deliveries, employee payroll, customer relationships, and day-to-day operations in one system.

## What the System Covers

- fuel trip operations and dispatch
- fleet and driver administration
- payroll and statutory deductions
- employee records and workforce management
- customer and lead management
- invoices, payments, and revenue tracking
- operational alerts and business dashboards

## Why It Matters

The business needs a reliable way to monitor performance, reduce operational risk, and maintain compliance while keeping daily operations efficient. The system provides a single operational view for management, finance, HR, and dispatch teams.

## Key Benefits

- improved operational visibility
- faster trip and driver management
- better payroll accuracy
- stronger compliance monitoring
- cleaner customer and lead handling
- better financial oversight
- faster management decision-making

## Dashboard Focus

The dashboard presents the most important business metrics at a glance, including:

- active trucks
- fuel delivered
- revenue generated
- trips in progress
- payroll summary
- alerts and operational issues

## Strategic Value

The platform is designed to support business growth by improving control, reporting, and operational consistency across the company.

## Future Direction

The next phase includes full authentication, database-backed operations, deeper reporting, and mobile support for field teams.

## Conclusion

Sarura Fuel Logistics is positioned as a modern operational platform for a growing fuel transport business, built to improve efficiency, visibility, and control across the company.
