# Sarura Fuel Logistics Management System

Sarura Fuel Logistics is a modern operations and business management platform designed for a Kenyan fuel transport company. The system brings together fleet operations, employee management, payroll processing, statutory compliance, customer relationship management, and business reporting in one streamlined environment.

## Business Purpose

Sarura supports the full operational lifecycle of a fuel logistics business, including:

- recruitment and employee management
- driver and truck allocation
- trip planning and delivery monitoring
- fuel loss tracking and operational oversight
- payroll, deductions, and statutory compliance
- lead generation and customer follow-up
- invoicing and payment tracking
- executive dashboards and alerts

## Core Features

### Operations
- trip creation and assignment
- driver and truck availability tracking
- route and customer management
- fuel loading and delivery monitoring
- proof-of-delivery capture
- abnormal fuel variance alerts
- trip status tracking

### Fleet and Compliance
- truck record management
- insurance, inspection, and licensing alerts
- maintenance scheduling
- driver document expiry monitoring
- compliance checks before trip assignment

### Human Resources and Payroll
- employee records and onboarding
- salary structures and allowances
- payroll run processing
- PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, and housing levy calculations
- deductions, loan recovery, and payslip generation
- payroll approval and payment export

### Sales and CRM
- lead tracking and follow-up
- customer profiles and credit rules
- quotation and order management
- conversion tracking and deal pipeline
- customer communication history

### Finance
- invoice generation
- payment tracking
- expense recording
- revenue and cost reporting
- accounts receivable and payable overview

### Reporting and Alerts
- dashboard KPIs
- operational summaries
- revenue and expense analysis
- alert center for compliance and business risks
- document expiry and overdue payment reminders

## Design Approach

The interface is designed to feel professional, reliable, and operationally focused. It uses a modern dashboard layout with:

- dark navy sidebar navigation
- orange highlight accents for key actions
- white cards on a light neutral background
- KPI tiles for operational metrics
- clean tables for trip, payroll, and customer data
- status badges for warnings, success, and risk states
